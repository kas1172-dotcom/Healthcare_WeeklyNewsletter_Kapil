The Pipeline

Four stages, fully automated

Every Monday morning a scheduled job runs the full pipeline on cloud infrastructure — no server to maintain, no button to press.

01

Ingest

Pulls from federal APIs (Federal Register, Congress.gov, openFDA), agency enforcement pages, and 20+ RSS feeds across regulatory, legal, and trade press.

02

Classify

Each item is tagged with a category, urgency level, and audience-specific analysis — turning raw notices into a structured, prioritized brief.

03

Synthesize

Generates an editor's note and a "theme of the week" for each edition, framing the noise into a narrative a busy professional can absorb in minutes.

04

Publish

Commits the result to the repository; the static site picks it up instantly. Readers filter by date range, sort by importance, and drill into any category.

The Inputs

Primary sources, not aggregators

Every item links back to the official record — the Federal Register entry, the OIG enforcement action, the committee bill. No second-hand summaries in the citation chain.

The Output

One pipeline, two audiences

The same underlying data is framed differently depending on who's reading — because a compliance officer and a legislative aide need different things from the same CMS rule.

Edition 01

Consulting

Operational and advisory framing — what a regulatory change means for clients, compliance posture, revenue cycle, and market positioning. Categories span enforcement, coding, HIPAA, devices, telehealth, and M&A.

For: strategy consultants · compliance officers · health system executives
Edition 02

Policy

Legislative and analytical framing — what's moving through Congress, what the evidence base says, and where payment reform is heading. Categories span legislation, payment models, health equity, drug pricing, and workforce.

For: legislative staff · lobbyists · government affairs · policy researchers
